Methodology

Excluded categories: AI pitch-deck reviewers and NDA & deal-room tools — these compete on a single feature, not on BizMatch's combined founder-matching + marketplace surface. Remaining categories (Direct competitors, Cofounder-matching platforms, Capital & discovery marketplaces) were ranked independently by each ranker's own default preset, the top 10 from each list were pulled, and the three lists were merged and de-duplicated below.
