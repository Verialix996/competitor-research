Registration Flow

Research date: 2026-07-19. Screenshots are sanitized public copies from the private registration-flow capture set; credentials, tokens, verification links/codes, browser chrome, and private identifiers are not intentionally published.

Outcome: stopped at CAPTCHA / Cloudflare / anti-bot verification.

Stop point: Security-check error on the combined signup/login page, after submitting the authorized email and before any password, verification message, account creation, or onboarding step

Blocker / boundary: AngelList's reCAPTCHA-backed security check could not verify the automated browser session after the authorized email was submitted. This is an anti-bot control, so the flow was stopped without bypass or retry.

  1. Official public homepage. AngelList's official homepage presents its private-funds platform, including Venture Funds, SPVs, Scout Funds, and Digital Subscriptions. The public navigation offers Sign in and Contact sales; no separate public Sign up link is shown.
  2. Combined signup or login entry. The official Sign in link opens venture.angellist.com/v/login, headed Sign up or log in to AngelList. The blank entry form accepts an email address and offers Continue, with Google as an alternative authentication route and links to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  3. Security-check blocker. Submitting the authorized registration email did not advance to password creation or onboarding. The page displayed an error stating that its security check could not verify the session and suggested refreshing, disabling extensions, or trying a new incognito window. The page's console identified the bot-detection strategy as reCAPTCHA, so the flow was stopped without retrying or attempting to bypass the control.
