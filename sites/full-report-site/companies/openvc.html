Strategic Positioning

What it is
OpenVC is primarily a searchable investor database and fundraising platform that helps founders find, filter and approach relevant investors.
Primary job solved
Find, filter and contact relevant investors
Product model
Searchable investor database with investor-fit information, filters, outreach support, and fundraising-readiness tools.
BizMatch lesson
Provide searchable investor fit and actionable outreach context.
Do not copy
Do not make a static investor list the center of BizMatch.
Important clarification
Primarily an investor database and outreach tool, not mutual matching or a match-to-collaboration platform.
