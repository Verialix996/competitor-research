Single Source Of Truth

The canonical source for active research is data/competitive-research-tracker.csv. The generated site data file canonical-data.js, the table, company profiles, category pages, priority competitor page, and strategic conclusions are generated from that file plus the explicit strategic classification rules in tools/generate_site.py.

Older files under reports/ and data/bizmatch-competitive-research-cited.* are archived context only and should not be used as active facts when they conflict with the canonical tracker.

Research Limitations

  • Some traction data is self-reported by the companies.
  • Registered users are not the same as active users.
  • Company funding is different from capital raised through a platform.
  • Prices may change after the checked date.
  • Not found does not mean a capability does not exist.
  • Secondary-source information is marked as lower-confidence context where applicable.
  • Data behind a paywall is not treated as fully verified.

Capability Verification Model

Capabilities are displayed as Confirmed, Partial, Not found, or Not applicable. Confirmed requires direct support in the canonical tracker's cited notes. Partial is used for limited analogs or marketing-level evidence. Not found is transparent uncertainty, not a negative factual claim.
